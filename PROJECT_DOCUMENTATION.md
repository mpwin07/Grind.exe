# grind.exe ⚔️ — What is it and How it Works

Welcome to **grind.exe**! This is your ultimate personal study partner for coding interviews. It is designed with a cool, futuristic cyberpunk theme to make your practice feel like a game and help you build a consistent coding habit.

---

## 💡 What does this app do?

Instead of tracking your practice on spreadsheets, **grind.exe** connects to your LeetCode profile to automatically gather your statistics, analyze your strengths and weaknesses, reward your consistency, and help you get faster at solving coding problems.

---

## 🌟 Main Features

### 1. Main Dashboard (Home Screen)
*   **Automatic Progress Updates**: Shows your ranking, reputation, and how many coding problems you have solved.
*   **Experience Points (XP) & Levels**: You gain points and level up as you solve more problems, just like in a video game!
*   **Difficulty Ring**: A colorful ring showing your progress split across Easy (green), Medium (yellow), and Hard (red) questions.
*   **Weekly Habit Check**: A quick view of the current week showing which days you solved problems to help you stay on track.

### 2. Consistency & Achievement Badges (Streaks)
*   **Streak Tracker**: Tracks how many days in a row you have solved at least one coding problem.
*   **Achievement Milestones**: Earn special reward badges when you maintain your habit for 3 days, 7 days, 30 days, or 100 days.

### 3. AI Mode (Your Personal Coding Tutor)
*   **Weakness Detector**: The AI analyzes your past practice to find areas where you need improvement.
*   **Custom Practice Sets**: Based on your weaknesses, the AI recommends a targeted list of 9 real coding problems to practice.
*   **AI Solution Finder**:
    *   Generates side-by-side solutions showing a **Brute Force** (simple first attempt) and **Optimized** (fastest/best performance) approach.
    *   **Language Selector**: Instantly switches code between languages like Java, Python, C++, and JavaScript.
    *   **One-Click Copy**: A single button to copy the solution code so you can test it immediately.

### 4. Time Analytics (Your Speed Tracker)
*   **Automated Sync**: Connects to LeetCode to fetch your latest solved problems automatically. You don't need to manually type in what you solved.
*   **Smart Time Tracker**: Calculates how long you spent on each question. If you solve problems close together, it calculates the exact minutes; otherwise, it assigns a realistic estimate based on the difficulty.
*   **Daily & Monthly Charts**: Shows beautiful graphs of your daily practice time, how many minutes you average per difficulty, and your weekly trends.
*   **12-Month Heatmap**: A calendar grid showing your consistency over the past year. The darker the green box, the more time you spent coding on that day.
*   **Personal Bests & Insights**: Displays your fastest solve, slowest solve, and helpful weekly summaries (like: "You solved Medium questions 15% faster this week!").

### 5. Settings Screen
*   **Link Profile**: Put in your LeetCode username to load your stats.
*   **Customize Style**: Tweak the colors of the theme to personalize your dashboard accent colors.
*   **Reset Data**: Clean up or clear your history at any time.

---

## 🚀 How to use it

1.  Open the website in your browser.
2.  Type in your LeetCode username when prompted.
3.  Go to **AI Mode** to get a custom recommended list of problems to solve.
4.  Solve them on LeetCode.
5.  Go to the **Analytics** page to see your solve times, charts, and heatmaps update automatically!
